Where Students Are

How does participation differ across states and territories?

Most states are within a few points of each other. The ACT leads; Tasmania trails.

Who Gets Access?

Examining differences between student groups.

Access differs by gender, remoteness and state. Gender gaps by age are small (under 4 pp).

Gender Gap in Study Participation

Women minus men, currently enrolled in study, by age group, 2025.

Key finding: Women are ahead at every age except 20–24. The largest gap is ≈3.9 pp at 25–29.

What could affect this?

Possible factors, not findings from this data.

  • Men may be more likely to take apprenticeships and trades.
  • Women may be more likely to enrol at university.
  • Caring duties may limit study in later years.

Work and Study Engagement by Remoteness

People aged 15–24. Each square is 1% of the area's young people. Group percentages, not individual transitions.

Key finding: Major Cities 84% fully engaged; regional areas 75%.

Gender Gap in Study Enrolment by State

Women minus men, in percentage points (pp), 2025. One hexagon per state, laid out like Australia. Darker means a larger gap.

Key finding: The NT has the largest gap (+5.1 pp) and Victoria the smallest (+0.3 pp). Small populations (NT, ACT, TAS) make their figures less stable.
